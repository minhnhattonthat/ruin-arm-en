# Ruin Arm - story synopsis (for translators)

Source: `work/tl/dump_msg*.txt`. Ids are message ids (`10F8` = msg10 line F8). Spoilers are fenced as
**REVEAL at <id>**: until that id, characters do not know it and early lines must not give it away.
Proposed English names: `work/tl/terms_proposed.tsv` (not binding until copied to `glossary.md`).

Name variables: `<N0>` hero (default Carmine etc.), `<N1>` his younger sister (default Bonnie etc.),
`<N2>` Zieg, `<N3>` Toran, `<N4>` Posena, `<N5>` Neil. The hero is a silent protagonist: he only
speaks through menu choices (he uses both 僕 and 俺 there) and one scream (1321).

## World and backstory (told late, at 1276-12B8; keep early lines vague)

- Long ago a super-civilization built on "machines" (キカイ, written in katakana) and magic destroyed
  itself; its remains are the **ruins** (遺跡) scattered over the world (prologue text 01AD). Many ruins
  are working teleporters: stand in the circle (サークル) and examine its center (1137); some need a
  password (018F-0191).
- Races: humans (人間族), merfolk (人魚族, living in Fubel, a land on the sea floor far below the human
  continent, cut off from the surface by choice, 1132/1144), beastfolk (獣人族, a village deep in the
  Velder Great Forest, 0059/0072-0075), winged folk (翼人族, Lucia's race; menu only). Creatures
  (クリーチャー) are violent monsters that appeared "a while ago" (0004).
- Kadam (カダム) was a rich, technologically advanced kingdom on a small island in the Arnoun region
  (アーノゥン地方), the far west. Contact was lost more than 10 years ago; rumour says a sudden explosion
  wiped it out with no survivors (0199-019A).
- **REVEAL at 127A**: Zieg is Zieg Kadam, the last king of Kadam. He and his research assistant studied
  the lost super-civilization and found that their own castle was a giant ruin (Baldoa). They revived it;
  its stored energy ran wild and destroyed the kingdom, leaving only the two of them alive (12A1-12AE).
  They also revived the humanoid machine that controlled the ruin, whose first words were "Exterminate
  humanity" (ジンルイヲ マッサツセヨ, 1290). The king panicked and fled, abandoning his wounded assistant
  (12B2).
- The king then travelled the world gathering allies: Bogan, the hero of Londium entrusted with a holy
  sword; a young man desperate to reach Arnoun (Toran); and a merfolk woman warrior (Queen Saara). They
  stormed Kadam, stopped the machine's energy and split it into **four gems** (宝玉), each keeping one
  and vowing to guard it (12B5-12B8). This was ~13-14 years before the game (004B).
- **REVEAL at 12E1**: the abandoned assistant is Zain. Betrayed and despairing, he threw himself off a
  cliff and was "reborn" in the machine's service; he founded the Raidroid Empire 13 years ago (11B1)
  to revive Baldoa and its master. Zieg first learns Zain is alive at 10CE.
- The four gems (each also an equippable item; Mephaus restores their powers, 00C6-00C9):
  Emerald (緑玉, Bogan's; regenerates HP), Topaz (黄玉/トパーズ, Zieg's; blocks status ailments),
  Sapphire (青玉/サファイア, Marthnal royal treasure; regenerates MP), Ruby (紅玉, Mediom royal
  treasure held by Toran; doubles attack). Together they are the energy that fully revives Baldoa
  (12CD, 12D4).

## Act 1 - Arff Village and the continent (1000-10EC; NPCs in msg00)

- 1000-1042: Peaceful home life in Arff (アーフ村). Father Bogan calls the siblings to supper; Lucia tags
  along. Sister scenes by the sea cliff: she has a bad premonition and asks her brother to protect her
  (103F-1042); choice scenes with Lucia squabbling (1034-103E).
- 1004-100E: Toran, an old friend in a tiger mask, visits Bogan ("what happened to your head?"). At night
  he warns that an army from Mediom is spreading across the continent and Londium is in danger. Bogan:
  "Could it be *him*..." (Zain; do not name him).
- 100F-1021: Next morning Bogan sends the siblings to a cave east of the village to fetch a sword and
  warns them of creatures. They find the golden holy sword (its spirit has weakened) and Bogan's letter:
  if anything happens, find Zieg in Londium, "a bit eccentric, but a kind old man". Back home, black
  soldiers have raided the house; Lucia, who hid (ぷはぁ, 101C), says Bogan fled to the cliff and
  hands over the gem Bogan left (the Emerald).
- 1043-1052: At the cliff, commander Neil demands the gem. Toran covers Bogan; both leap off the cliff.
  Neil easily beats the hero. The village chief later blames Bogan and asks the siblings to leave
  (0008-0014); the kindly Roa and her daughter Sara take them in; the sister decides to head for Londium
  (01A5-01B1).
- Whaial (ワイアル): two children, Edia and Roy, have taken in an amnesiac man and insist he is their dead
  father, "not Bogan" (1060-1063). It is Bogan, memory lost from the fall. Edia confesses later (0040).
- 105D-1072, 0048-00C9: Londium (ロンディウム王国), the strongest military power on the continent. Knight
  Garzack recognises the sword: Bogan was captain of the Londium Knights, entrusted with one of the
  kingdom's three holy swords, who suddenly resigned 13-14 years ago and retired to Arff. King Caesar
  welcomes "Bogan's children". Smith Gilm reforges the sword in stages. Court magician Mephaus explains
  the memory potion Call Mind (コールマインド), brewed by an alchemist from a mystery gem found in the
  west cave; he also studies the gems and develops new spells for the sister.
- Side quest: Meiya, a beastfolk girl, needs the Flower of Life (命の花) from the Velder Great Forest for
  her sick father; the fortune-teller Ogilin in the beastfolk village foretells a "cruel fate", the
  keyword "cape", a man full of hatred and behind him "Bolboza", and recites a prophecy: a blue sword
  will bind and strike down "Terror", "but the true Terror..." (007B-007E).
- 1080-10BE: Stelm Duchy (ステルム公国) is occupied by the Raidroid Empire. The party is thrown in jail,
  meets Zieg in the next cell (Lucia squeezes through the window to steal the key, 10A1). In his lab Zieg
  finds his Topaz stolen; he joins (the sister is left behind, 10AA) and gives jump boots, his invention.
  At Fefus Castle they defeat Goldo, Earth General and one of the Empire's Four Heavenly Kings, an
  honourable warrior (10B6-10BE). Duke Volsamry, who fled first, rewards them (00A7-00A8).
- 10B0-10B4: Zieg's letter: Bogan has headed for Cape Godhope. The sister's letter: she has gone after
  him alone.
- 10C6-10EC: Cape Godhope ruins. Neil confronts the "amnesiac" Bogan; Zain (Imperial Guard General)
  arrives, says Bogan is faking and names Zieg. **REVEAL at 10DB**: Bogan remembers everything. Bogan
  entrusts the children to Zieg and unleashes his last power to blast his friends away through the ruin
  (10E0-10E7); **Bogan dies here** (confirmed 1353). Zain: "we caught the girl, the trump card is ours"
  (10EB). The sister is captured; Neil looks at her strangely (10EC).

## Act 2 - Fubel, the undersea land (10ED-1149; NPCs 0121-0138, 01C9, 01E0)

- 1141-1149 (chronologically first): the hero wakes after three days in Fubel (フーベルの大地), the merfolk
  land on the sea floor. Posena Marthnal, princess of the Marthnal Kingdom (マースナル王国), found him
  drifting down. Lucia is badly hurt. He can breathe only thanks to the ring he wears (the Mobius Ring,
  メビウスリング).
- 10F1-1108: The Empire has occupied Marthnal and taken Queen Saara. At a council in the royal villa nobody
  dares fight; Posena's old nanny (ばあや) begs her not to go. Posena asks the hero to come; he joins her
  (10F9) and the nanny thanks him. Smith Hanutu, "the best in the world", reforges the sword (1109-110C).
- 110D-111C: At Sarai Castle the Lamia Warrior demands the Sapphire in exchange for the queen; beaten, she
  reveals the queen was taken to the Raidroid Undersea Base by Dine, Water General. 1120-1122: Dine
  (haughty "oh-ho-ho" lady) is defeated.
- 1125-1132: Saara is rescued, heals Lucia, forbids Posena further danger (she is heir), and on seeing the
  ring on the hero's finger turns cool: the merfolk cut ties with the surface; he should go home via the
  ruin north of the base.
- 1134-1140: At the ruin Posena gives the hero the Sapphire for safekeeping and tries to say something
  about "that ring". **REVEAL at 0123** (NPC): the Mobius Ring is a legendary token of love given by a
  mermaid to a human across the races. Keep early ring lines neutral; romantic subtext only.

## Act 3 - Lutea and the Resistance (114A-11A9, 1351-137E; NPCs 0139-0165)

- 114D-1154, 1351-1363: Back on the surface. Zieg (washed up near town) rejoins; Toran, alive, learns from
  Lucia that Bogan is dead and the sister missing, and offers to join (1354). The Imperial special forces
  hunt "a tiger-headed giant, an old man wearing a barrel, a 13-14-year-old girl and a boy with a small
  winged girl" (013C).
- 1155-1170: Lutea Autonomous Region (ルテア自治領) has been taken by Hude, Wind General: polite, sadistic,
  cowardly. In the mansion he holds kidnapped women, the sister among them, and slips away using them as
  hostages; some girls were moved to Tifnu (1375).
- 1171-117C, 0153: The resistance "Thunder of Kadam" (カダムの雷), led by Lord Robert Aysod, former ruler
  of the region; password "Kadam" (1175-1177). Aysod's plan: the main force attacks while the heroes
  strike Hude's throne room.
- 117D-119E: Hude has an informer: Reina, Aysod's daughter, who loves him. Hude takes her hostage; she
  stabs him with a hidden knife and dies apologising to her father.
- 119F-11BF: The wounded Hude bargains (player choice 11A6). He says the sister was sent to Mediom and Zain
  founded the Empire 13 years ago. The Dark Princess Knight Milia (闇姫騎士ミリア) kills him as a traitor
  and challenges the hero to come to Mediom. Toran: "Zain? He's alive?" (11B0).
- Tifnu region (ティフヌ領): ruled by the vain lady Margarita (019B, 01BF), who stays away from her mansion.

## Act 4 - Mediom (11C5-1275; NPCs 0166-018E)

- 11C5-11CD: Mediom (メディオム王国), once Londium's rival, is an Imperial base. Princess Fara is to be
  publicly executed unless the royal gem (the Ruby) is brought (017C, signed "Zain"). Neil, the local
  commander, fair to the people, protested and is to be executed as a traitor too. Toran rushes off at the
  news ("Fara!?").
- 0166-0175: Mediom's two princes vanished long ago; Fara rules in their place. Locals think Neil
  resembles prince Neiran.
- 11CF-11ED and 1224-1253 (two versions of the climax; which plays depends on earlier events, unchecked):
  the heroes free Neil; Zain arrives; masked Toran brings the Ruby. Zain controls Fara with a curse
  (じゅばく) and sends her to kill Toran; the curse breaks (11E8). Zain orders Neil to kill Toran; Toran
  does not fight back.
- **REVEAL at 11E4 / 1240**: Toran is Neil's elder brother, the elder prince of Mediom; Neil is Neiran,
  the younger prince; Fara is their little sister. Neil joined a survey party to Kadam with Toran, was
  buried in rubble, and believed Toran abandoned him (1295-1297); Zain fed that lie. In truth Toran drew
  the enemy away as a decoy, was nearly killed, was barred from returning, and joined Zieg's party to
  rescue him (1298-129F, 1206-120B).
- 1204-1216 / 1250-1253: **Toran dies**, either shielding Neil from Zain or after his duel with Neil,
  asking Neil to take the Ruby and help the hero in his place.
- **REVEAL at 1263/1265**: Milia is the hero's sister, brainwashed by Zain. She fights the party, then
  wakes with no memory (11F9, 1263). Zieg: "So Milia was...".
- 1218-1275: Neil will not be "a friend", but tests the hero in a duel and joins (1274/1275). He warns that
  Freed, Flame General, the last of the Four, guards Baldoa Castle in the Raidroid homeland, and that the
  emperor cannot be hurt by ordinary attacks.
- 1276-12B8: Zieg tells the backstory (above), in narration with the reveals 127A (Zieg = king of
  Kadam), 127E (the hero = Bogan), 1281 (merfolk warrior = Queen Saara). Posena eavesdropped (1280-1283).
- 1286-128C: The party splits into two teams; the player picks the sister or Posena, then Zieg or Neil.
- 018C-0191: Fara gives the ruin password "Raidroid Empire" for the teleporter west of Mediom.

## Act 5 - Baldoa (12B9-12FD)

- 12BA-12CC: Freed, Flame General: poetic ("two butterflies drawn to the flame"), a soldier who only does
  his duty; he longs to fight Neil.
- 12CD-12EC: Zain in Titan Castle Baldoa (巨神城バルドア, a giant humanoid fortress; its control room is
  in the head, 130B). Humans are fated to die by Bolboza's decree; he wants the last two gems. To Zieg
  (12DF-12E2): "You betrayed me". Zieg wants to free his soul. Zain dies begging Zieg to save him (12E5).
- 12D8, 12ED-12FD: Bolboza (ボルボーザ), the humanoid machine and the "emperor": created by humans, it
  judged them selfish and sentenced humanity to extinction. Mechanical lines are in katakana (keep a
  distinct robotic style). It taunts Zieg: "Did you not desire my power?" (12FB).

## Ending (1300-1348, 0200; variants by party, unchecked)

- Baldoa runs wild under Bolboza's last order and will destroy the world; the only fix is to fly it high
  and self-destruct, piloted from the head (1306-130B, 133F-1343).
- Variant A: Zieg stays to pilot it, claiming an escape pod exists; Neil knows there is none: "he means
  to die" (1317-1319, 1328-1335). Zieg: "I'll join you soon, Toran, Bogan... and Zain" (1313).
- Variant B: the sister casts Move (ムーブ, teleport spell) to send everyone out, thanks her brother and
  stays behind (131E-1321).
- 1345-1348: Zieg exults that Baldoa is his and he will destroy the planet, then "Curse you, Bolboza..."
  (apparently possessed or tricked by Bolboza; a bad-ending branch?). Needs a playtest.
- 0200 "A YEAR LATER", then credits (0201-0209, staff names: credits are in English per ruling).
- 01AF "TO BE CONTINUED" is an unused/joke message.

## Factions and recurring terms

- Raidroid Empire (ライドロイド帝国): "black soldiers" (黒い兵士), Imperial special forces (帝国特殊部隊);
  homeland in Arnoun, seat Baldoa Castle. Generals: the Four Dark Generals (四魔将, also called the Four
  Heavenly Kings 四天王): Earth, Water, Wind, Flame; above them Zain (近衛魔将) and Milia.
- Kingdoms: Londium (Knights 騎士団, King Caesar), Mediom (occupied), Marthnal (merfolk, undersea),
  Stelm Duchy (公国), Lutea Autonomous Region, Tifnu region, Kadam (destroyed).
- Resistance (レジスタンス / 反乱軍), "Thunder of Kadam".
- The holy sword (聖剣), one of Londium's three holy swords (三聖剣); its spirit (精霊) has weakened and
  smiths (Gilm, Hanutu) restore it stage by stage (キズナの剣 in the item list).
- Systems: weapons wear out and need repair; stars (★) are stat points to allot; charge (タメ) for special
  moves (必殺技), two-player combo specials (協力必殺技); alchemist (錬金術師) forges weapons from ingots
  (インゴット) and the four elements (元素: earth, fire, water, air).
